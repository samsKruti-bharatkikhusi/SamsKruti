# Aklavya — The First-Time Visitor Persona

**SamsKruti · India's Cultural Discovery Platform**
_Ideal Customer Persona (ICP) framework, mapped to Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs._

> Source: `Aklavya-Maslow-Framework.docx` (downloaded 2026-07-15), captured verbatim into the project docs.

---

## 1. Who is Aklavya

Aklavya is SamsKruti's Ideal Customer Persona (ICP): a **first-time visitor to Varanasi**, entering the city as a tourist with no prior knowledge of it. He does not know the streets, the customs, or where to begin — the platform's entire entry experience is designed around what he needs the moment he arrives.

**Questions he has to answer**
- Why is he here?
- What exactly does he want to do?
- Purpose of him being here?

**What we know about him**
- He should be here to explore the city
- He has to be treated as a tourist
- First-time visitor
- Does not know anything about the city

## 2. His 11 Needs

Starting from the original 8 questions on the ground, expanded to close the gaps around **safety, etiquette, and connectivity** — the anxieties that sit beneath budget, stay, and travel but are just as decisive for a first-time visitor.

1. Budget I have?
2. Is it safe? What scams should I watch out for?
3. Where to stay?
4. How to travel?
5. Where to go? (with best time / season for each spot)
6. Etiquette — what NOT to do (temple dress code, ghat sensitivity, photography rules)
7. What to eat? (+ water / food hygiene)
8. Connectivity & language — SIM, network, will people understand me?
9. What are the pictures I can click?
10. What are the experiences I want to have?
11. What are the things I can buy? (+ cash vs UPI, ATMs)

## 3. Mapped to Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs

Reading his needs through Maslow's five tiers shows where the platform must simply **function as a utility**, and where it gets to become the **emotional, story-led experience** SamsKruti is built to deliver.

| Maslow tier | Aklavya's needs at this tier |
| --- | --- |
| **Self-Actualization** — purpose, transformation, meaning | Where to go · What experiences I want to have |
| **Esteem** — recognition, achievement, self-image | Pictures I can click · Things to buy · Famous tastes I want to have |
| **Love & Belonging** — fitting in, connection, not feeling like an outsider | Etiquette (what NOT to do) · Language (will people understand me) |
| **Safety** — security, stability, protection | Budget · Is it safe / scams to avoid · How to travel · Connectivity (SIM, network) · Money logistics (cash vs UPI, ATMs) |
| **Physiological** — basic survival | Where to stay · What to eat (+ water / food hygiene) |

## 4. What This Means for SamsKruti

The three storytelling segments — **Sheher Ki Galiyan, Khane Ki Parampara, Sheher Ka Hriday** — live almost entirely at the top two tiers: **Esteem** and **Self-Actualization**. Aklavya cannot get there emotionally until the bottom three tiers (Physiological, Safety, Belonging) are handled first.

**Segment alignment**
- **Sheher Ki Galiyan** (Streets of the City) → Self-Actualization: where to go, the experiences he came for
- **Khane Ki Parampara** (Food Tradition) → Esteem / Self-Actualization boundary: the famous tastes he wants to have
- **Sheher Ka Hriday** (Heart of the City) → Esteem: pictures, things to buy, artisan stories
- **Etiquette + Language** (Belonging) → not a content segment, but a trust layer beneath all three

**The gap**
Physiological, Safety, and Belonging needs are **not currently owned by any of the three segments**. This is the likely reason field research on parking, entry logistics, and queue timing (e.g. Kal Bhairav, Kashi Vishwanath) keeps surfacing as a blocker — these are tier-2 logistics questions standing between Aklavya and the tier-5 experience SamsKruti is trying to sell.

**Recommendation:** a lightweight **"before you arrive" utility layer** — covering stay, safety, connectivity, and etiquette — sitting underneath the three storytelling segments. This lets Sheher Ki Galiyan, Khane Ki Parampara, and Sheher Ka Hriday stay purely emotional and immersive, while the utility layer quietly clears the path to get Aklavya there.

## How this connects to the guide workbook _(editorial note, not from the source doc)_

**Aklavya *is* the traveler the 3-day guidebook is being built for** — the first-time visitor who "does not want to feel lost." This framework maps almost 1:1 onto the work in flight:

- The **practical layer** we added to every step (`how · cost · tip`) and the **arrival-time personalization** are exactly the **Safety / Physiological** tiers — the utility that clears Aklavya's path.
- The **maker stories, photo plates, and the map** are the **Esteem / Self-Actualization** tiers — the emotional payoff.
- The **"lost? scan / call" lifeline** and the (planned) **language layer** address **Belonging** — never an outsider, never stranded.
- **The gap the doc names** (stay, safety, connectivity, etiquette not owned by any segment) is a strong candidate for its own **guidebook section / "before you arrive" spread** — a natural addition to the locked skeleton (Front · Days · Souls · Keep-it · Back).
