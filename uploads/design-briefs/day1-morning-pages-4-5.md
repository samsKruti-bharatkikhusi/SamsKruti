# Design Brief (open) — Varanasi Guide Workbook · Day 1 · Morning

_A brief for the UI designer. This gives you the **content, the intent, and the audience** — **not** a fixed layout. Design it however serves the reader best. The current app build is only a rough draft; **do not feel bound by it.**_

---

## 1. What this is

- **Product:** SamsKruti "Varanasi · Three Days" — a **guide workbook**. Think a beautiful **printed 8.5×8.5-inch square book** (also a digital reader). A keepsake the traveller lives inside: they **tick** things off, **paste** their own photos, and **scan** codes to go deeper.
- **Who it's for — "Aklavya":** a **first-time visitor to Varanasi** who knows *nothing* — not the lanes, not the customs, not where to begin. Quietly anxious. (Full persona: `uploads/product-documentation/aklavya-persona-framework.md`.)
- **The arc the book gives him:** *anxious outsider → confident → moved → it becomes his own.* Handle his **fear** first (safety, logistics, etiquette), then let him feel the **wonder**, then let him **capture** it.
- **This spread = Day 1 · Morning:** a **pre-dawn temple pilgrimage** (he wakes ~3:15 AM and moves through the sleeping city). Mood: **sacred, hushed, unhurried, a little awe.** Not a busy dashboard — a calm, reverent morning.

## 2. What these two pages must *achieve* (the job — you decide the look)

Two facing pages. The intent is fixed; the design is open.

- **Left page — "The places I'll go":** the instant he opens the morning, he should **relax** — *"these are where I'm going, I recognise them, and the whole story is one scan away."* It carries the **six places** (name, a one-line essence, an image), and a **scanner** to the full stories.
- **Right page — "The plan":** he should feel **"I know exactly where I go and how"** — the **route (a map)** and the **schedule** (times, in order, tickable).

That's the only rule: **pictures on the left, the plan + map on the right.** Everything else — composition, hierarchy, how the map looks, how the cards sit — is yours.

## 3. THE FULL MORNING PLAN (the raw material)

_Source: `SamsKruti_Varanasi_Itinerary.html` (Day 1 · Slot 1). Draft — the source flags many items for on-ground field verification (below). Use as much or as little as the design wants; extra spreads are welcome if it helps Aklavya._

**Slot theme:** _"Seek Permission. Receive Blessings. Begin Your Journey."_
**Group size:** 4–6 founding members.

### Before leaving the hotel
- **Wear:** comfortable cotton clothes · easy-to-remove footwear · light shawl/jacket (winter).
- **Carry:** government ID · cash ₹500–₹2,000 in small notes · water bottle · power bank · hand sanitiser.
- **Leave behind:** large bags/backpacks · sharp objects · tobacco or alcohol · unnecessary electronics.

### The route
Hotel → Kal Bhairav → Maha Mrityunjaya → Kashi Vishwanath → Corridor → Vishalakshi → Breakfast → Vishwanath Gali. _(All stops sit close together in the old-city cluster around Kashi Vishwanath — a small area, walkable.)_

### The stops, in detail

**1 · Kal Bhairav Temple** — 4:00–4:30 AM
- Purpose: the traditional **guardian of Kashi**; pilgrims **seek His permission** before visiting Vishwanath.
- Time: 20–30 min · Crowd: usually light at 4 AM · Footwear: remove outside.
- Offerings: flowers, prasad, (oil — confirm local practice).

**2 · Maha Mrityunjaya Temple** — 4:30–4:50 AM
- Purpose: revered Shiva temple of the **Mahamrityunjaya mantra** (against death).
- Time: 15–20 min · Crowd: lighter than Kashi Vishwanath.

**3 · Kashi Vishwanath Temple** — 5:15–7:15 AM
- The main **Jyotirlinga darshan** + **Annapurna Temple** + the temple complex.
- Time: 1.5–2 hours · Footwear: outside / locker · **Electronics: not permitted inside — lockers at the entrance.**

**· Vishwanath Corridor** — 7:15–7:40 AM
- Walk the restored corridor — ghat views, temple perspectives, open space to pause. 20–30 min.

**4 · Shri Vishalakshi Temple** — 7:40–8:00 AM
- ~3 min walk from the corridor · 15–20 min · one of the **51 Shakti Peethas**.

**· Banarasi breakfast** — 8:00–9:00 AM
- Must try: kachori-sabzi · jalebi · lassi · Banarasi chai. Budget ₹150–300 pp.

**· Vishwanath Gali** — 9:00–10:30 AM
- Explore: Banarasi silk · rudraksha · brass · Gulabi Meenakari · wooden toys · mithai · puja items.
- Note: **browse today, buy at the end of Day 2** (after seeing everything).

### The six places — name + one-line essence (for the "places" page)
1. **Kal Bhairav** — the city's guardian; seek His permission first.
2. **Maha Mrityunjaya** — the temple of the deathless mantra.
3. **Kashi Vishwanath** — the golden Jyotirlinga, Kashi's heart.
4. **Annapurna Temple** — the goddess who feeds all of Kashi.
5. **Vishwanath Corridor** — the restored path down to the river.
6. **Vishalakshi** — one of the 51 Shakti Peethas.

### The scanner (on the "places" page)
A QR that opens the **full story of each temple** — why you go, in what spirit, and what to do inside. (One scan for all, or one per place — your call.)

## 4. Brand reference (guidance, not a cage)

Use as a starting palette/voice — improve freely.

- **Feel:** a warm, printed **craft-book / travel journal**. Ivory paper, gold + terracotta accents, deep-brown ink.
- **Type:** an elegant **serif** (currently Cormorant Garamond) for English; a **Devanagari serif** (Noto Serif Devanagari) for the Hindi lines that head each section (e.g. `ये जगहें`, `पहला दिन · प्रातः`).
- **Format:** a **8.5×8.5-inch square** book; these two pages are a facing spread (reads 2:1 open).
- **Workbook cues:** things are **tickable**, photos are **paste-able**, codes are **scannable** — the reader makes the book their own.

## 5. The open brief

> Design **Page 4 (the places)** and **Page 5 (the plan + map)** as one facing spread of an **8.5×8.5-inch square** guide-workbook for Aklavya — a first-time pilgrim, moving through Kashi before dawn. Make him feel *oriented, unhurried, and quietly moved*. You have the full morning above; use what serves him. The only fixed rule is **pictures left, plan + map right** — everything else, including how the map is drawn and how the six places and the schedule are composed, is yours to design.
