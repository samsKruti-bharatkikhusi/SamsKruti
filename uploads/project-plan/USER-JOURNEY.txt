 SAMSKRUTI — THE USER JOURNEY  (the perfect plan)
 No application. Just: a book + a campaign + a guided journey.
 The traveller's emotional arc: LOST -> FOUND -> GUIDED -> MOVED -> TELLS OTHERS
 Last updated: 2026-07-12
================================================================

 For each stage:  what happens · what THEY feel · what YOU do · what it SCORES.

----------------------------------------------------------------
 1. LOST            (before — the pain that opens everything)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Happens: they're planning a Varanasi trip, drowning in a hundred
           videos, blogs, conflicting advice.
  They feel: overwhelmed, anxious — "where do I even go? what if I
             waste this trip?"
  You do:  nothing yet — but THIS pain is your whole opening line.
  Scores:  — (this is the wound your product heals)

----------------------------------------------------------------
 2. FOUND YOU       (discovery — THE CAMPAIGN)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Happens: they meet your message where they already are —
           Instagram / YouTube / Reddit / FB travel groups / Google
           ("Varanasi itinerary") / a friend who went.
  They feel: "wait, this is exactly my problem."
  You do:  show up where Varanasi-bound travellers gather, with ONE
           honest line: "Going to Varanasi? Lost in a hundred videos?
           Let me guide you." No hype. Just the pain, named.
  Scores:  a hand raised / attention.

----------------------------------------------------------------
 3. FIRST TASTE     (the FREE 1-2 pager — trust begins)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Happens: they tap -> simple landing -> answer 3 questions
           (how long? · what pulls you? · what pace?) -> receive a
           free, soulful mini guided plan.
  They feel: relief + "oh — this person actually GETS it."
  You do:  ask the 3 questions; write/deliver the free pager (by hand
           at first — you ARE the guide).
  Scores:  a GUIDED SOUL  <- the north star.

----------------------------------------------------------------
 4. THE YES         (the BOOK offer — the business)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Happens: having felt the free quality, they're offered the full
           beautiful guidebook (~Rs.500) — whole trip day-by-day,
           every page a soul, phone number inside.
  They feel: "obviously yes" — trust is already earned; Rs.500 is
             less than one trip dinner.
  You do:  make the offer; produce the book.
  Scores:  a BOOK SALE  <- the business proof.

----------------------------------------------------------------
 5. THE JOURNEY     (in the city — the REAL win, value delivered)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Happens: they arrive with the book, walk the days, cross-check as
           they go; when lost or unsure -> they call the number ->
           a real guide helps LIVE.
  They feel: never alone. "A guide is walking beside me."
  You do:  BE the phone guide; refine the itinerary from what they
           actually hit. (Every call teaches the next edition.)
  Scores:  the value itself — the whole reason this exists.

----------------------------------------------------------------
 6. THE GLOW + TELL (after — the GROWTH engine)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Happens: moved by the trip; the beautiful book comes home as a
           keepsake; friends see it and ask; they recommend it.
  They feel: proud, grateful — they want others to feel this too.
  You do:  ask the two questions (did it genuinely guide you? would
           you send a friend?); make the book beautiful enough to
           show off. The book keeps selling in living rooms.
  Scores:  a RECOMMENDATION  <- 2nd half of the north star.

----------------------------------------------------------------
 7. THE LOOP        (the flywheel)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Their recommendation IS the next traveller's Stage 2.
  When recommendations-per-soul > 1, it grows on its own.

================================================================
 THE REAL WIN (layered — smallest to biggest)
   - Micro:    one soul says "I felt guided, never lost."
   - Business: they paid Rs.500 gladly.
   - Growth:   they sent a friend, for free.
   - Flywheel: >1 recommendation per soul -> it grows without you pushing.

 YOUR PROCESS (the ~1hr/day engine that powers all 7 stages)
   - RESEARCH      -> fills the content (Phase A)
   - WRITE plans   -> the guiding (Phases B/C)
   - PRODUCE book  -> the product (Phase D)
   - RUN campaign  -> the discovery (Stage 2)
   - BE the guide  -> live value + learning (Stage 5)
   - TRACK score   -> souls / sales / recommendations (GUIDED-SOULS.txt)

 The journey is the WHY. The build plan (PROJECT-PLAN.txt, Phases A-E)
 is the HOW. Same thing, two views.
