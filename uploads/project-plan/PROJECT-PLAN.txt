THE REAL PROBLEM (locked)
  A traveller enters a city and feels LOST — a hundred videos, no
  idea where to go or what to do. We make them feel GUIDED, like a
  guide is walking beside them. That feeling is the whole product.

NORTH STAR (next 6 months)
  100 genuinely guided souls  +  how many recommend it onward.
  (Second number = book sales: the business proof. Never let selling
   override guiding — guided souls stay the true star.)

THE ONE RULE
  Finish ONE thing to DONE before opening the next. At ~1hr/day,
  spreading across many things finishes nothing. Depth beats spread.
  And: build UP from the real problem — never DOWN from the dream
  (no brand/team/enterprise/scale before the lost traveller is served).

WHAT WE ARE BUILDING (the funnel)
  1. FREE 1-2 pager — "Going to Varanasi? Felt lost in a hundred
     videos? Tap -> get a guided experience." (the taste / the hook)
  2. THE GUIDEBOOK (~Rs.400-500) — beautiful, every page a soul; the
     whole trip day-by-day (1/2/10 days) in the traveller's hand.
  3. THE PHONE NUMBER inside — call a real guide when lost. Never
     stranded. (Founder IS the guide/AI, for now.)

PARKED (deliberately, not abandoned)
  [~] The BOX + shop + build-your-own + box-3d — too much work, unclear
      demand, it's a gift not self-use. Its RESEARCH (the 5 crafts,
      makers, lanes, coords) carries over into the guidebook/itinerary.
  [ ] The immersive AI app (enter-the-city, clock starts) — the
      destination; earned later, built from the first 100 souls.
  [ ] Cart / checkout / payments.
  [ ] Other segments (Galiyan, Swaad) and other cities.

 PHASE A — Deep research of Varanasi        [NOW · the foundation]
----------------------------------------------------------------
  The content of the book AND the itinerary. Do it for a REAL trip,
  never abstract. Core question to answer, truthfully and deeply:
  "One / two / ten days, someone trusting me completely — where do I
   take them, in what ORDER, and WHY?"  (The 'why' is the guiding.)
  [ ] The spine: a day, hour by hour (dawn ghats -> ... -> night)
  [ ] The stops: places, crafts (5 already researched), food, people
  [ ] The 'why' for each stop (what only a local who loves it knows)
  [ ] The lost-moments: where people get confused -> what to tell them

----------------------------------------------------------------
 PHASE B — The itinerary framework          [the guided logic]
----------------------------------------------------------------
  [ ] The 3 questions: how long? · what pulls you? · what pace?
  [ ] How answers -> a personalised day-by-day plan
  [ ] Make it feel like a friend walking beside them, not a listicle
